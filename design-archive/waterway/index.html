Waterway at Grandview

Custom waterfront living in the heart of Sneads Ferry

Tucked along the water in Sneads Ferry, NC, this coastal neighborhood was designed for a more intentional way of living—where luxury feels effortless and every day begins with the calm of the coast. Mornings start slow here, with coffee on the porch, boats drifting by, and soft light reflecting off the water. Thoughtfully crafted homes, a peaceful waterfront setting, and easy access to boating, beaches, and coastal dining create a lifestyle that feels both elevated and relaxed. It’s the kind of place where refined design meets laid-back coastal charm, offering a rare opportunity to enjoy waterfront living at its best.

Meet the builder

Built on the coast, for the coast

Creating a custom home can seem complex, but with OTBX Custom Builders and our Coastal Dream Home Advantage, the process becomes clear, steady, and as relaxing as life along the North Carolina coast should be.

With over 30 years of experience building custom homes along the coast of North Carolina, Danny Spell, the owner of OTBX Custom Builders, is renowned for his unwavering commitment to quality craftsmanship and client satisfaction. As a veteran-owned business, Danny brings a disciplined and detail-oriented approach to every project.

Danny's unique approach revolutionizes home building by proving that luxury and affordability can coexist. He insists on using top-tier materials and custom features, from hand-crafted cabinets to premium stone countertops, without compromising on cost-effectiveness. Danny's process is designed to deliver a superior finished product that stands the test of time while still respecting budget considerations. He guides clients towards smart, lasting choices that blend quality and value, demonstrating that with his expertise, homeowners truly can have it all—exquisite craftsmanship and financial prudence in perfect harmony.
