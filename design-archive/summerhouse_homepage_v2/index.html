About the community

Discover the difference

Nestled along the Intracoastal in the growing town of Holly Ridge, Summerhouse is made for an active life on the water. Boating, cycling, fishing, or an afternoon in the sun — the community was planned around all of it.

Scenic trails, day docks, protected fishing water and a resort-style pool sit inside one gated neighborhood, minutes from Topsail's beaches and half an hour from downtown Wilmington.
