Community fact sheet / 03 · Coastal North Carolina

Redd’s Cove

Holly Ridge, North Carolina · Canal and community water access

A boating-oriented community with canal-front opportunities and a flexible build timeline. The supplied Tackle Box contains the following lot and construction guidelines.

Community at a glance
HOA
$1,800 per year per supplied Tackle Box
Build timing
No required timeframe to build
Minimum home
1,800 sq. ft.
Site limits
Impervious surface: 4,600 sq. ft.; maximum height: 45 ft.
Setbacks
Front: 25 ft.; sides: 10 ft.; rear: 15 ft.
Waterfront rule
AEC line is 30 ft. from water on waterfront lots; no building within the line
Canal / docks
Canal dredged to 5 ft. at low tide; dock may extend up to 25% of canal width. Tackle Box estimates 15–20 ft. into an average 75-ft. canal; gazebos allowed on docks; two boats per dock
Utilities / costs
Community-septic lots: $250/year deferred-maintenance charge; each lot pays for a grinder pump (roughly $6,000)
Closing
Tackle Box says cash buyers have no closing costs and no working-capital contribution; financed buyers may have about $1,400 in attorney fees
Buyer profile

Best fit: buyers who want water access and flexibility on when to build, and are comfortable reviewing waterfront construction constraints carefully.

Agent verification notes

Flood zone shown as VE in the supplied Tackle Box. Verify current HOA documents, survey, AEC line, flood determination, dock permit feasibility, utility costs and all closing figures for the specific lot.

Prepared for DRG agent reference. Community rules, availability, fees, amenities and property-specific terms can change; verify with current governing documents and the applicable listing/developer before advising a buyer.
