Salters Haven at Lea Marina

Hampstead, North Carolina · Marina-centered coastal community

A boating-focused coastal community where marina ownership, resident slip rentals and golf-cart-friendly living are part of the lifestyle. Builder requirements and slip terms vary, so confirm details for each lot or home.

Community at a glance
HOA
$1,760 per year; HOA has been turned over to the residents.
Marina slip dues
$1,760 per year, separate from HOA dues.
Slip ownership / rental
Salters Haven lot owners and homeowners may purchase a slip. Slip owners may rent their slip to other Salters Haven residents.
Boat storage
Gated boat storage at the back of the community; $1 per foot for residents. Confirm billing period and current rules.
Builder requirements
Interior lots must be built by Legacy Bill Clark Homes or Atlantic Homes. In the custom section by the marina, buyers may use any builder.
Minimum home size
1,800 sq. ft. minimum.
Community governance
HOA has been turned over to residents.
Lifestyle
Golf-cart community.
Amenities
Community information previously supplied lists a marina, pool, clubhouse, kayak launch, outdoor fire pits and other outdoor amenities. Confirm current availability and access rules.
Setting
Hampstead, North Carolina, along the Intracoastal Waterway.
Buyer profile

Best fit: buyers who want a waterfront-community lifestyle with a separately owned marina slip, resident rental flexibility and convenient boat storage.

Agent verification notes

Confirm the current HOA budget, marina slip dues and purchase process, slip rental rules, boat-storage billing period, and builder requirements for the specific lot type.

Custom-section lots by the marina allow any builder; interior lots are limited to Legacy Bill Clark Homes or Atlantic Homes.

Prepared for DRG agent reference. Verify community rules, fees, availability and property-specific terms with current governing documents before advising a buyer.
