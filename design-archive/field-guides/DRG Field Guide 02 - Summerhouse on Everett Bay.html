Community fact sheet / 02 · Coastal North Carolina

Summerhouse on Everett Bay

Holly Ridge, North Carolina · Gated waterfront community

A waterfront community built around outdoor recreation, shared amenities and access to the water. Lot requirements and flood conditions vary by location, so confirm each homesite’s details before advising a buyer.

Community at a glance
HOA
$1,800 per year
Setting
Gated waterfront community on Everett Bay, with waterfront, marsh-view and interior homesites.
Amenities
Basketball court, billiard room, clubhouse, community pool, fitness center, pickleball, playground, tennis courts, trails, street lights, RV/boat storage and private golf carts permitted.
Water access
Community boat ramp and day docks are included. ICW-front lots cannot have a private boat dock, per supplied details; confirm applicable restrictions for each lot.
Minimum home size
2,000 sq. ft. in the front section; 2,400 sq. ft. in the remainder of the community. Confirm which standard applies to the specific homesite.
Flood zones
AE and VE flood zones are present in areas near the water. Verify the current FEMA flood designation for each parcel.
Roads / storage
Road maintenance and RV/boat storage are listed among the HOA/neighborhood amenities. Confirm storage availability, fees and current rules.
Homes / builders
Custom and semi-custom homesite opportunities; builder participation and current availability should be confirmed.
Location
Holly Ridge, North Carolina, convenient to Topsail Island and the greater Wilmington area.
Buyer profile

Best fit: buyers seeking a gated community with a broad amenity package, water access, outdoor recreation and a coastal neighborhood setting.

Agent verification notes

Confirm current HOA budget and rules, the applicable minimum square footage by section, exact flood zone, and dock restrictions for the specific parcel. Community amenity availability, storage fees and builder participation may change.

Prepared for DRG agent reference. Verify community rules, fees, availability and property-specific terms with current governing documents before advising a buyer.
