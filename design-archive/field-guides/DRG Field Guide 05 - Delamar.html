Community fact sheet / 05 · Coastal North Carolina

Delamar

Hampstead, North Carolina · Gated coastal community

A gated coastal community with custom-home requirements, managed private roads, extensive recreation amenities and defined ARC guidelines. Verify all property-specific details against current governing documents.

Community at a glance
Developer / marketing
Developer: Delamar NC, LLC. Marketing company: Destination Realty Corporation.
HOA / management
Delamar Homeowner’s Association, Inc. HOA management: CAMS. HOA dues: $205/month ($2,460/year).
Builder / timing
Blue Crest Homes is the only builder identified in supplied notes. No required timeframe to begin building after purchase; builder must complete construction and landscaping within 550 days of ARC approval.
Home / height
Minimum 2,300 sq. ft. (limited areas noted). Maximum building height in supplied notes: 35 ft. First floor must be 32 in. above finished grade.
Utilities
Pender County Utilities for water/sewer; natural gas through Piedmont Natural Gas; electricity through Duke Energy; Spectrum/Charter cable/telephone. Underground fiber-optic internet per supplied community notes.
Roads / flood
Paved private roads maintained by the HOA. Community described in supplied notes as outside a flood zone; verify current flood determination for each parcel.
Amenities
Gated community; clubhouse/fitness center; pool; lighted tennis and pickleball courts; boat storage facility; community lake with lighted walking trails; two fishing piers; stocked pond; asphalt walking trails; private community boat ramp; private beach/community waterfront area; beach, gas grills, restrooms and park.
Boating
Boat storage: $100/month per supplied notes. Eight private slips currently noted as available; total dock count TBD. Confirm slip availability, rights and terms.
Schools
North Topsail Elementary, Surf City Middle School and Topsail High School (per supplied document; verify current assignment).
Setbacks — Phase 1
Lots 1–39: 20 ft. from front property line; 25 ft. rear; 5 ft. each side; 15 ft. each corner property line.
Setbacks — Phase 2
Lots 40–88: 30 ft. from front property line; 25 ft. rear; 10 ft. each side; 15 ft. each corner property line. ARC may require alternate setbacks. Confirm phase and lot-specific rules; supplied notes separately identify waterfront setbacks of 30 ft. front, 25 ft. rear and 10 ft. sides.
Site / fencing
Impervious surface: 4,340+ sq. ft. per supplied notes. Black aluminum fencing per supplied notes. Confirm ARC standards and lot-specific impervious-surface limits.
Fees / contributions
AC fee: $2,000 per supplied document. Capital contribution: $2,500 per supplied notes. Compliance security: refundable $7,500 plus $4,000 non-refundable Road Access Fee payable to Delamar Homeowners Association. Confirm amounts and timing before closing.
Taxes
Document lists 2024–2025 combined Pender County and Hampstead Fire District rate of $0.8325 per $100 valuation; example: $4,162.50/year on $500,000. Rates may change.
Closing / attorney
Preferred developer-lot attorney listed: Law Office of J. Chris Huff, 1650 Military Cutoff Rd., Suite 202, Wilmington, NC 28403; phone 910-251-2273. Document says earnest money checks should be payable to the law office.
