About the community

What is Salters Haven?

Nestled along the sparkling Intracoastal, in the growing town of Hampstead, NC, Salters Haven is a place for those who seek an active lifestyle on the water.

Whether you're a boating enthusiast, avid cyclist, fishing aficionado, or simply love to bask in the sun and swim, this neighborhood caters to all your coastal desires. With pristine fishing spots and sparkling swimming areas, every moment spent here is filled with adventure and relaxation.
